Nami Island Travel Guide: Getting There, Where to Eat and Local Tips

Last updated: November 25, 2026

한국어 · English · 日本語 · 中文

About this place

A small island in the Bukhan River, a popular day trip for its metasequoia avenue and four-season scenery. Known as a K-drama filming location, it draws many international visitors.

Local tips

🚇 Getting there

Take the Gyeongchun Line ITX-Cheongchun or local train to Gapyeong Stn, then a taxi (about 10 minutes) to the Nami Island pier. Sangbong to Gapyeong takes about an hour.

🗺️ Suggested course

Gapyeong Stn → ferry to the island → walk the metasequoia and ginkgo lanes → back to Gapyeong Stn. A day trip is enough.
