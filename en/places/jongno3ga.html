Jongno 3-ga old-school alleys Travel Guide: Getting There, Where to Eat and Local Tips

Last updated: November 23, 2026

한국어 · English · 日本語 · 中文

About this place

The Jongno 3-ga area gathers long-running eateries, pojangmacha alleys and the Nakwon instrument arcade, a classic Seoul "nopo" district. Expect gukbap, grilled fish and makgeolli with loyal regulars. Some menus are Korean-only or cash-only, so bring a translation app and cash.

💡 Visiting tip

Right by Jongno 3-ga Stn (Lines 1, 3, 5). Some menus are Korean-only, so have a translation app ready. Combine it with the Ikseon-dong hanok alleys next door.

Local tips

🚇 Getting there

The old-eatery lanes start right outside Jongno 3-ga Stn (Lines 1, 3, 5). Ikseon-dong and Nakwon Arcade are 3–5 minutes on foot.

🗺️ Suggested course

Evening in the pocha alleys of Jongno 3-ga → Nakwon Arcade → dessert at a cafe in Ikseon-dong → subway home from Jongno 3-ga.

☕ Cafe locals like

익선동 한옥 카페 골목 — The hanok alleys of Ikseon-dong have plenty of cafes for after-dinner coffee. Some old eateries are cash only, so carry cash. View on map ↗

Where to eat: menus & prices

  • Jongno 3-ga pojangmacha street 종로3가 포장마차 거리 ↗
    Must-orderSpicy chicken feet, grilled eel, seafood with sojuPrice range₩20,000–30,000 per dish

    After dark, orange tents line the street — Seoul’s classic outdoor drinking scene. It’s the best way to feel Jongno 3-ga, named Time Out’s “coolest neighbourhood in the world” for 2026.

  • Oyster bossam alley (e.g. Samhaejip) 굴보쌈 골목 (삼해집 등) ↗
    Must-orderBoiled pork with fresh oysters, pork-bone stewPrice rangeSmall oyster bossam about ₩30,000

    Boiled pork wrapped with raw oysters and kimchi — a winter favorite, best from November to February.

  • Galmaegisal (pork skirt) alley 갈매기살 골목 ↗
    Must-orderCharcoal-grilled pork skirtPrice rangeAround ₩10,000-something per portion (varies)

    Small BBQ joints spill tables into the alley, where office workers and travelers sit side by side — pure Jongno 3-ga.

Prices are rough figures from online menus and reviews as of November 2026. They vary by shop and season, so check the menu on site.
