How to enjoy stylish Seoul

  • Namsan Cable Car: Myeong-dong Stn Exit 3 → about 15 min on foot. Adults ₩15,000 round trip, 10:00–23:00.
  • Seoul Sky view: Go up an hour before sunset for both sunset and night views. Adults about ₩30,000-something.
  • Han River night cruise: From Yeouido pier (Yeouinaru Stn). Standard tickets ₩10,000–29,000.
  • Ttukseom drone show & picnic: The 2026 drone shows ended on Oct 31; picnics work year-round. Right by Jayang Stn (Line 7).
  • Naksan Park × KPop Demon Hunters: The setting of Rumi and Jinu’s night scene. Start from Hyehwa Stn and time it for sunset.

Olive Young flagship stores by area

  • Seoul Myeong-dong · Olive Young Myeongdong Town: A global flagship designed for international shoppers (renovated Nov 2023).
  • Seoul Seongsu · Olive Young N Seongsu: Five floors, Korea’s largest store; about 70% of sales come from international visitors.
  • Seoul Hongdae: The area with the most stores, including the two-floor Hongdae L7 branch, all within walking distance.
  • Seoul Gangnam · Olive Young Central Gangnam Town: Offers a personal-shopper service for international customers.
  • Busan · Seomyeon / Gwangbok-dong (Nampo) / Haeundae: Stores cluster in Busan’s three main shopping districts; find the nearest one on the map.
  • Jeju · Yeon-dong & Nohyeong (New Jeju): Jeju saw the fastest growth in foreign shoppers; stores cluster in New Jeju near the airport.

Shopping by budget

  • Under ₩10,000: Sheet masks (₩1,000–3,000 each), lip balms, minis and snacks like konjac jelly — great for gifts.
  • ₩10,000–30,000: Sunscreen, toner pads, serums, cleansers and lip tints — the range travelers buy most.
  • ₩30,000 and up: Ampoules and skin boosters, value sets, beauty devices and inner-beauty supplements. Cheaper during Olive Young sales.

What international shoppers buy

  • Sunscreen: Beauty of Joseon Relief Sun: Loved for its light, moisturizer-like feel with little white cast or shine.
  • Serum: Torriden Dive-In serum: A hyaluronic-acid hydrating serum that keeps getting recommended.
  • Sheet masks: Mediheal: Many types at low prices — popular for bulk buys and gifts.
  • Skin boosters: VT Reedle Shot, Medicube PDRN: The social-media darlings. Start with a low-strength version if you’re new to them.
  • Snacks: Delight Project bagel chips, konjac jelly: Healthy snacks especially popular with Japanese and Southeast Asian travelers.

Half-day shopping routes

  • Myeong-dong route: Myeong-dong Stn → Olive Young Myeongdong Town → street-food stalls → Shinsegae Duty Free Myeongdong, 11F K-WAVE Zone (K-pop goods).
  • Seongsu route: Seoul Forest Stn → KWANGYA@SEOUL (SM goods) → Olive Young N Seongsu → pop-up stores on Yeonmujang-gil → café break.
  • Hongdae route: Hongik Univ. Stn → Hongdae Olive Young stores → busking on Hongdae’s main street → Gyeongui Line Forest Park in Yeonnam-dong.

Shopping tips

  • Bring your passport: Many stores offer instant tax refunds at checkout when you show your passport; ask in-store about conditions.
  • Also try Daiso and Musinsa: In 2026 international shoppers flock to Olive Young, Daiso and Musinsa — Daiso for bargain cosmetics and goods, Musinsa for Korean fashion brands.

Renting hiking gear: Seoul Hiking Tourism Centers

  • Bukhansan center (closed Mon): 5 minutes’ walk from Bukhansan Ui Stn (Ui-Sinseol Line), near the Baegundae trailhead.
  • Bugaksan center (closed Tue): 88 Samcheong-ro, Jongno-gu. Good for the city-wall and Cheong Wa Dae hill trails; also lends raincoats and camping chairs.
  • Gwanaksan center (closed Wed): B1 inside Gwanaksan Stn (Sillim Line), 23 Sillim-ro. Rent gear and start hiking straight off the train.

What to bring by season

  • Spring & autumn: Big day-night temperature swings — layer up. Start early on autumn weekends to beat the crowds.
  • Summer: At least 1 liter of water, a hat and insect repellent. In the rainy season (late June–July) streams can rise suddenly.
  • Winter: Crampons, gloves and a thermos. Winter sunset is around 5–5:30 p.m., so head down by 3 p.m. at the latest.
  • Check trail closures: Some trails close during wildfire-prevention periods (spring, autumn) or bad weather. Check the Korea National Park Service site.

From screen to street

  • Bukchon ↔ KPop Demon Hunters “Free” rooftop scene; Lovely Runner’s well: A residential area with visiting hours (Mon–Sat 10:00–17:00).
  • Naksan Park wall ↔ Rumi & Jinu’s night scene: Most film-like right after sunset.
  • N Seoul Tower ↔ the final concert: The stadium in the film is fictional.
  • Myeong-dong ↔ Saja Boys’ “Soda Pop” debut: Combine with Olive Young Myeongdong Town.
  • Seoul Sky ↔ HUNTR/X’s home: In reality it’s the observatory from the 117th floor up.
  • Gyeongbokgung ↔ Lovely Runner, The Matchmakers: Free entry when wearing hanbok.
  • Insadong ↔ the film Insadong Scandal: Wander the antique shops and galleries.

Half-day routes (map)

  • Palace & hanok route (4–5 hours): Rent hanbok near Gyeongbokgung Stn → Gyeongbokgung (free entry, guard ceremony) → Bukchon → Insadong Ssamziegil → dinner in Ikseon-dong.
  • KPop Demon Hunters walk (about 4 hours, start afternoon): Bukchon → Donhwamun-ro by Changdeokgung → Hyehwa Stn → Ihwa Mural Village → sunset and night views on the Naksan wall.

Hanbok rental

  • Free palace entry: Wear traditional hanbok (jacket plus skirt or trousers) for free entry to the four main palaces and Jongmyo. Some fusion styles may not qualify.
  • Prices and where: Rental shops cluster around Gyeongbokgung Stn Exits 3–4 and the Bukchon entrance. A few hours usually costs ₩15,000–25,000; hair styling and accessories are extra.
  • Hanboknam Gyeongbokgung: One of the well-known shops near the palace, offering traditional and themed hanbok. Booking online in advance is often cheaper.

K-pop goods shops

  • KWANGYA@SEOUL (Seongsu): SM Entertainment’s official flagship, B1 of Acro Seoul Forest D Tower, linked to Seoul Forest Stn. Albums, light sticks, photocards and more.
  • Shinsegae Duty Free Myeongdong K-WAVE Zone: A K-pop corner on the 11th floor with goods from BTS, BLACKPINK, BIGBANG, NCT and more. As a duty-free store, you need your passport and departure details.
  • K-pop shops in Myeong-dong: Several shops along Myeong-dong sell albums and merch to a mostly international crowd. Compare prices and check authenticity.
  • Seoul My Soul Shop: The city’s official souvenir shop, with branches at the Myeong-dong Tourist Information Center and Sejong Center.

Market tips

  • Carry a little cash: Most places take cards, but some stalls are cash-only. A few ₩10,000 notes are plenty.
  • Share and sample: Sample 2–3 stalls instead of filling up at one. Some shops require one dish per person, so ask.
