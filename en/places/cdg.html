Changdeokgung Palace Travel Guide: Getting There, Where to Eat and Local Tips

Last updated: November 25, 2026

한국어 · English · 日本語 · 中文

About this place

A UNESCO World Heritage palace whose buildings follow the natural terrain. The rear garden (Huwon) is visited by timed tours, so check the details in advance.

Local tips

🚇 Getting there

Walk about 5–10 minutes from Anguk Stn (Line 3), Exit 3, to Donhwamun gate. The Secret Garden is a guided, timed visit, so check times and booking. Closed Mondays.

🗺️ Suggested course

Secret Garden tour → Ikseon-dong (10 minutes on foot) → dinner among the old eateries near Jongno 3-ga Stn.

☕ Cafe locals like

익선동 한옥 카페 골목 — Ikseon-dong has cafes and restaurants in converted hanok. It is quiet by day and crowded in the evening. View on map ↗

Please check opening status and hours on Google Maps before you go.

On screen

  • DramaKingdom (2019– )

    Changdeokgung's rear garden (Huwon) is known as a key filming location for the Netflix series "Kingdom." Many scenes were also shot alongside neighboring Changgyeonggung.

  • DramaThe Red Sleeve (2021)

    Several scenes were reportedly filmed at Buyongji Pond in the rear garden. The area around the pond is a popular photo spot during a palace visit.
