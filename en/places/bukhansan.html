Bukhansan Travel Guide: Getting There, Where to Eat and Local Tips

Last updated: November 23, 2026

한국어 · English · 日本語 · 中文

About this place

A national park on Seoul's northern edge, reachable by subway and bus. Baegundae (about 836 m) gives a view of the whole city, and gentler dulle-gil trails fit any fitness level.

Local tips

🚇 Getting there

From Bukhansanuii Stn (Ui-Sinseol Line) it is a 10–15 minute walk to the Ui-dong ranger station. Allow about 5–6 hours round trip for Baegundae.

🗺️ Suggested course

Beginners can walk an easy dulle-gil section; fit hikers can continue to Baegundae. Eat in Ui-dong afterwards and return on the Ui-Sinseol Line.

☕ Cafe locals like

우이동 계곡 카페·식당가 — Cafes and restaurants cluster at the Ui-dong entrance for after the hike. Bring hiking shoes, water, and finish before sunset. View on map ↗
