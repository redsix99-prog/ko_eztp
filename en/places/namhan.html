Namhansanseong Fortress Travel Guide: Getting There, Where to Eat and Local Tips

Last updated: November 25, 2026

한국어 · English · 日本語 · 中文

About this place

A mountain fortress about an hour from Seoul, listed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2014. The wall walk is gentle and popular in autumn colors.

Local tips

🚇 Getting there

From Sanseong Stn (Line 8), Exit 2, take bus 9 up to Namhansanseong (about 25 minutes). Check the timetable in a map app.

🗺️ Suggested course

South Gate → walk part of the wall (2–3 hours) → Haenggung palace → lunch at the restaurants inside the fortress.

☕ Cafe locals like

남한산성 행궁 인근 전통찻집 — Inside the fortress you will find traditional tea houses and hanok cafes. Autumn weekends are very crowded, so weekdays are better. View on map ↗
