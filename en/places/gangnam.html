Gangnam Station Travel Guide: Getting There, Where to Eat and Local Tips

Last updated: November 23, 2026

한국어 · English · 日本語 · 中文

About this place

The intersection at Gangnam Station (Subway Line 2) is one of Seoul's biggest shopping and beauty strips, anchored by a huge Olive Young store and brand flagship shops. It connects to an underground mall and back-alley dining streets, good for a half-day loop.

💡 Visiting tip

Store hours and the brand lineup change often, so it's worth checking before you go.

Local tips

🚇 Getting there

Exit 10 of Gangnam Stn (Line 2) is closest to the Olive Young town. The Sinbundang Line also connects here.

🗺️ Suggested course

K-beauty shopping at Olive Young → browse the underground mall → dinner in the alleys behind Exit 11 → the shopping area continues toward Sinnonhyeon Stn.

☕ Cafe locals like

강남역 11번 출구 뒷골목 카페 — The alleys behind Exit 11 have plenty of quiet cafes for a break. It is calmer there than on the main Gangnam-daero street. View on map ↗

Please check opening status and hours on Google Maps before you go.

How to do it

Olive Young Gangnam Town

Near Exit 10 of Gangnam Station, this large Olive Young store splits skincare, makeup and perfume by floor, and offers skin or personal-color consultations.

In-store instant refund

Many shops offer an in-store instant tax refund above a set amount. Bring your passport and check the airport refund process before departure too.

Underground mall and dining alleys

The underground mall connected to the station makes shopping possible even on rainy days, and the back alleys off Gangnam-daero are packed with restaurants and bars.
