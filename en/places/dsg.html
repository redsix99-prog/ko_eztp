Deoksugung Palace Travel Guide: Getting There, Where to Eat and Local Tips

Last updated: November 23, 2026

한국어 · English · 日本語 · 中文

About this place

An easy-to-reach palace by City Hall where traditional buildings stand beside Western-style ones. The stone-wall road next to it is a popular stroll.

Local tips

🚇 Getting there

Exit 2 of City Hall Stn (Lines 1 and 2) puts Daehanmun gate in sight. Closed on Mondays.

🗺️ Suggested course

Deoksugung Palace → stone wall road and Jeongdong-gil → Seoul Museum of Art → walk on to Cheonggyecheon (about 10 minutes from City Hall).

☕ Cafe locals like

정동길 · 덕수궁 돌담길 카페 — Cafes and tea rooms in restored old buildings line Jeongdong-gil. The wall road is especially popular during the autumn ginkgo season. View on map ↗

Please check opening status and hours on Google Maps before you go.
