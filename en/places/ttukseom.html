Ttukseom Hangang Park Travel Guide: Getting There, Where to Eat and Local Tips

Last updated: November 23, 2026

한국어 · English · 日本語 · 中文

About this place

A Han River park near Seongsu-dong where you can sit in the river breeze. At sunset the lights across the river come on, and you can try the local "Han River ramyeon" and delivery-chicken picnic culture.

Local tips

🚇 Getting there

From Ttukseom Resort Stn (Line 7) it is about a 5-minute walk to the park. From Ttukseom or Seongsu Stn (Line 2) allow 15–20 minutes.

🗺️ Suggested course

Seongsu cafes and shops → Seoul Forest walk → sunset and Han River ramyeon at Ttukseom.

☕ Cafe locals like

어니언 성수 (Onion Seongsu) — A bakery cafe in a converted old Seongsu factory, steadily popular with locals. Go in the morning on weekends. View on map ↗

Please check opening status and hours on Google Maps before you go.

How to do it

Han River drone light show

In 2026 the free shows ran several times in April–June and September–October at the waterside stage in Ttukseom (8:30 p.m., about 15 minutes, up to 2,000 drones). This year’s regular season ended on October 31; 2027 dates will be posted on Seoul’s official drone-show site.

How to picnic on the Han

Bring a mat, claim a spot on the grass and try instant “Han River ramyeon” cooked at the park convenience store. Delivery food like fried chicken can be picked up at the park’s delivery zones. Please sort and take care of your trash.
