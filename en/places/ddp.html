Dongdaemun Design Plaza (DDP) Travel Guide: Getting There, Where to Eat and Local Tips

Last updated: November 23, 2026

한국어 · English · 日本語 · 中文

About this place

A curvy landmark building that hosts exhibitions and fashion events. Lit up at night, the exterior alone is worth seeing, and the nearby Dongdaemun fashion district is easy to combine.

💡 Visiting tip

Directly connected to Dongdaemun History & Culture Park Station (Lines 2, 4, 5).

Local tips

🚇 Getting there

Exit 1 of Dongdaemun History & Culture Park Stn (Lines 2, 4, 5) is right in front of DDP.

🗺️ Suggested course

DDP exterior and exhibition → Dongdaemun fashion malls → DDP lit up at night → two stops on Line 4 to Myeong-dong.

☕ Cafe locals like

동대문 광희동 골목 카페 — Small cafes sit in the Gwanghui-dong alleys across from DDP. The fashion malls stay open late and some wholesale markets open in the early hours. View on map ↗

Please check opening status and hours on Google Maps before you go.

How to do it

Check exhibitions and fashion events

DDP regularly hosts design exhibitions and fashion-week events. Check the official website for that week's schedule before you go.

Linked to Dongdaemun's fashion malls

The wholesale-focused Dongdaemun fashion malls are within walking distance, and many stay open overnight. Some buildings don't serve retail customers, so check the signs at the entrance.

Best for night views

Evening is the best time for photos, when the LED rose sculpture and the building's curved facade light up.
