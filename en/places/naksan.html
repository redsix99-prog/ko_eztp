Naksan Park Travel Guide: Getting There, Where to Eat and Local Tips

Last updated: November 23, 2026

한국어 · English · 日本語 · 中文

About this place

A walking trail along the Hanyangdoseong wall, known for sunsets and night views. The nearby Ihwa mural village can be combined.

Local tips

🚇 Getting there

From Hyehwa Stn (Line 4), Exit 2, walk 15–20 minutes uphill via Ihwa Village to Naksan Park.

🗺️ Suggested course

Hyehwa and Daehangno → Ihwa mural alleys → Naksan fortress wall → night view → walk down toward Dongdaemun and take Line 1 or 4.

☕ Cafe locals like

학림다방 (Hakrim Dabang) — A classic cafe in Hyehwa open since 1956, loved by locals and artists for its classical music and strong coffee. View on map ↗

Please check opening status and hours on Google Maps before you go.

How to do it

To see it like the film

From Hyehwa Stn (Line 4) Exit 2, walk up through Ihwa Mural Village for about 20 minutes. Arrive around sunset to catch the wall lights and N Seoul Tower lighting up. We show a real photo of the place instead of film stills for copyright reasons.
