COEX & Starfield Library Travel Guide: Getting There, Where to Eat and Local Tips

Last updated: November 23, 2026

한국어 · English · 日本語 · 中文

About this place

A large mall in Samseong-dong whose towering Starfield Library bookshelves are a photo hotspot. Shopping, exhibitions and an aquarium are all in one place.

💡 Visiting tip

Directly connected underground to Samseong Station (Line 2) and Bongeunsa Station (Line 9).

Local tips

🚇 Getting there

Samseong Stn (Line 2), Exits 5 and 6 lead straight into COEX Mall. Bongeunsa Stn (Line 9) is also close.

🗺️ Suggested course

COEX Mall and Starfield Library → Bongeunsa Temple across the road (about 10 minutes) → walk in Seonjeongneung park.

☕ Cafe locals like

선정릉 주변 카페 — The Seolleung and Samseong area has plenty of cafes popular with office workers. For quiet, try the alleys beside Seonjeongneung. View on map ↗

Please check opening status and hours on Google Maps before you go.

How to do it

Photo spot: Starfield Library

Starfield Library's roughly 13-meter-tall bookshelves make it a free, popular photo spot. Weekend afternoons get crowded.

Aquarium and shopping

The underground COEX Mall combines a large bookstore, an aquarium and a multiplex cinema, making it a good rainy-day option.

Check the convention calendar

COEX frequently hosts large expos and conventions, so it can get crowded during those periods. Check the schedule before visiting.
