Seongsu-dong Travel Guide: Getting There, Where to Eat and Local Tips

Last updated: November 23, 2026

한국어 · English · 日本語 · 中文

About this place

A neighborhood of cafes, select shops and pop-up stores in converted old factories and warehouses. It gets crowded on weekends, so start in the morning.

💡 Visiting tip

Wait times for popular pop-ups can get long on Wednesdays and weekends.

Local tips

🚇 Getting there

Exit 3 of Seongsu Stn (Line 2) is close to the cafe streets; Exit 8 of Ttukseom Stn is also handy. The two stations are one stop apart.

🗺️ Suggested course

Seongsu Stn → boutiques and pop-ups on Yeonmujang-gil → Daelim Changgo cafe → finish at Seoul Forest.

☕ Cafe locals like

대림창고 갤러리 카페 (Daelim Changgo) — A spacious gallery-style cafe in an old warehouse that represents Seongsu well. Weekend afternoons are crowded. View on map ↗

Please check opening status and hours on Google Maps before you go.

How to do it

Pop-up store hopping

Global brands and up-and-coming designers open pop-up stores here all the time — check Instagram or map-app reviews beforehand to see what's on that week.

Cafes and boutiques in old factories

Former printing houses and shoe factories have been converted into cafes and boutiques tucked into the alleys. Weekday mornings are relatively quiet.

Pair with Seoul Forest

Seongsu-dong is within walking distance of Seoul Forest, so a park stroll followed by a cafe stop makes an easy combo.
