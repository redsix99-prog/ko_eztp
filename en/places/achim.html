Garden of Morning Calm Travel Guide: Getting There, Where to Eat and Local Tips

Last updated: November 23, 2026

한국어 · English · 日本語 · 中文

About this place

A Korean-style garden in Gapyeong, Gyeonggi-do, with different flowers and trees each season. It is known for a winter light festival, so check the operating schedule.

Local tips

🚇 Getting there

Take the Gyeongchun Line to Gapyeong or Cheongpyeong Stn, then a taxi or local bus (e.g. bus 30-4 from Gapyeong; check the timetable). Allow about 1.5 hours each way from Seoul.

🗺️ Suggested course

Walk the garden (1–2 hours) → eat in Gapyeong town → it can be combined with Nami Island in one day.
