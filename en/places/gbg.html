Gyeongbokgung Palace Travel Guide: Getting There, Where to Eat and Local Tips

Last updated: November 23, 2026

한국어 · English · 日本語 · 中文

About this place

Built in 1395 as the main royal palace of the Joseon dynasty. Walking the axis from Gwanghwamun to Geunjeongjeon and Gyeonghoeru shows the scale of the old capital. Check official notices for hanbok-wearing perks.

Local tips

🚇 Getting there

Exit 5 of Gyeongbokgung Stn (Line 3) leads toward the Gwanghwamun gate. You can also walk from Gwanghwamun Stn (Line 5), Exit 2.

🗺️ Suggested course

Gwanghwamun → Geunjeongjeon and Gyeonghoeru → Seochon alleys to the west → Tongin Market → walk on to Bukchon. The palace is closed on Tuesdays.

☕ Cafe locals like

대오서점 (Daeo Bookstore) — A cafe in an old Seochon bookstore, good for a quiet rest. Check official notices for hanbok admission perks. View on map ↗

Please check opening status and hours on Google Maps before you go.
