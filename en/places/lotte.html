Lotte World Tower Travel Guide: Getting There, Where to Eat and Local Tips

Last updated: November 25, 2026

한국어 · English · 日本語 · 中文

About this place

A 555 m skyscraper whose upper-floor observatory, Seoul Sky, overlooks the Han River and downtown. The view varies a lot with weather and air quality.

Local tips

🚇 Getting there

Jamsil Stn (Lines 2 and 8) connects underground to Lotte World Mall. Enter Seoul Sky at the tower entrance.

🗺️ Suggested course

Lotte World Mall → Seoul Sky view → stroll around Seokchon Lake → dinner in Songridan-gil.

☕ Cafe locals like

송리단길 카페 골목(석촌호수 인근) — Songridan-gil (around Seokchon Stn, Line 8) is lined with independent cafes that locals like. View on map ↗

Please check opening status and hours on Google Maps before you go.

How to do it

The view from Seoul Sky

From the 117th–123rd floors you can see the Han River, Namsan and, on clear days, toward the west coast. Floor 118 has a glass floor. Go up an hour before sunset to catch day, sunset and night views in one visit. Adults about ₩30,000-something (2026; check the official site); open Sun–Thu 10:30–22:00, until 23:00 on Fri, Sat and the eve of holidays.

Getting there

Connected underground to Jamsil Stn (Lines 2, 8). Pair it with a walk around Seokchon Lake.
