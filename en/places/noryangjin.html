Noryangjin Fisheries Market Travel Guide: Getting There, Where to Eat and Local Tips

Last updated: November 25, 2026

한국어 · English · 日本語 · 中文

About this place

Right next to Noryangjin Station, this fish market lets you pick live seafood yourself and eat it fresh at an upstairs restaurant. The pre-dawn auction is a sight, but a daytime visit is plenty to browse and eat.

💡 Visiting tip

A 5-minute walk from Noryangjin Station (Lines 1, 9); the pre-dawn auction (around 1–3 a.m.) is the liveliest to watch. Closed every Sunday and on major holidays, so check the official site before visiting.

Local tips

🚇 Getting there

Exit 1 of Noryangjin Stn (Lines 1, 9) is a 5-minute walk to the market entrance. For the pre-dawn auction, go around 1–3 a.m.

🗺️ Suggested course

Pick your fish and have it prepped on the first floor → eat upstairs for a small table fee → afterwards, check out the cup-rice (컵밥) street near the station.

☕ Cafe locals like

노량진역 인근 카페 — Around Noryangjin Stn, known as a study-exam district, casual snack and cup-rice shops mix with simple cafes — a different vibe from the market itself. View on map ↗

Where to eat: menus & prices

  • Mixed sashimi to go 모듬 활어회 포장 ↗
    Must-orderSeasonal mixed sashimi platter (flounder, salmon, shrimp, etc.)Price rangeAbout ₩50,000–80,000 for 2–3 people (varies with the day's market price)

    Pick the fish you want and they'll clean and pack it on the spot. Bring it to a nearby restaurant, which charges a small "table fee" and provides side dishes and seating.

  • Second-floor dining hall 2층 식당가 ↗
    Must-orderSashimi + spicy fish stew setPrice rangeAbout ₩2,000–3,000 extra per person for the table fee

    Restaurants on the second floor let you eat the sashimi you just bought right away. Reviews on price and service vary, so check the menu board and prices first.

Prices are rough figures from online menus and reviews as of November 2026. They vary by shop and season, so check the menu on site.
