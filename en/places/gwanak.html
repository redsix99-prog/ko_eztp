Gwanaksan Travel Guide: Getting There, Where to Eat and Local Tips

Last updated: November 25, 2026

한국어 · English · 日本語 · 中文

About this place

A rocky mountain in southern Seoul, known for Yeonjudae near the summit. The climb is demanding, so it suits experienced hikers; you can start from several directions.

Local tips

🚇 Getting there

From Sadang Stn (Lines 2 and 4) or Seoul Nat'l Univ. Stn (Line 2), walk or take a bus to a trailhead. There are several routes, so check the trailhead in a map app.

🗺️ Suggested course

The climb to Yeonjudae near the summit is steep. If you are short of time, walk a shorter route and come back down.
