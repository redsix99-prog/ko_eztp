Tongin Market Travel Guide: Getting There, Where to Eat and Local Tips

Last updated: November 25, 2026

한국어 · English · 日本語 · 中文

About this place

A cozy market in Seochon, west of Gyeongbokgung. Its dosirak cafe, where you buy side dishes with old-style coins, made it famous.

💡 Visiting tip

In Seochon just west of Gyeongbokgung, an easy walk after the palace (about 10 min from Exit 2, Gyeongbokgung Stn, Line 3).

Local tips

🚇 Getting there

It is about a 10-minute walk from Gyeongbokgung Stn (Line 3), Exit 2. The market lies in the Seochon alleys west of the palace.

🗺️ Suggested course

Gyeongbokgung → Seochon alleys → yeopjeon lunch box at Tongin Market → a Seochon cafe → stroll toward Inwangsan. The lunch-box cafe is busy at noon.

☕ Cafe locals like

효자베이커리 (Hyoja Bakery) — A long-running bakery on the road toward the former Blue House. Seochon has many other charming cafes as well. View on map ↗

Please check opening status and hours on Google Maps before you go.

Where to eat: menus & prices

  • Tongin Dosirak Cafe (coin lunchbox) 통인시장 도시락카페 (엽전 도시락) ↗
    Must-orderBuild your own lunchbox with old-style coinsPrice range1 coin = ₩500; 10 coins ₩5,000 (₩5,000–10,000 per person)

    Buy coins and an empty tray at the cafe, then fill it at stalls showing the sign. Many stalls close by mid-afternoon, so go at lunchtime.

  • Gireum tteokbokki (oil-fried rice cakes) 기름떡볶이 ↗
    Must-orderSoy-sauce or chili flavorPrice rangeA few coins (around ₩2,000)

    Stir-fried in oil with no sauce, crisp outside and chewy inside — Tongin’s must-try snack.

Prices are rough figures from online menus and reviews as of November 2026. They vary by shop and season, so check the menu on site.
