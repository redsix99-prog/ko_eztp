Dobongsan Travel Guide: Getting There, Where to Eat and Local Tips

Last updated: November 25, 2026

한국어 · English · 日本語 · 中文

About this place

A rocky mountain connected to Bukhansan with striking pointed peaks. Dobongsan Station leads straight to the trailhead, but check shoes and weather for the rocky sections.

Local tips

🚇 Getting there

From Dobongsan Stn (Lines 1 and 7) it is a 15–20 minute walk to the trailhead, passing hiking shops and restaurants.

🗺️ Suggested course

Seoul Iris Garden → trailhead → choose a route to suit your fitness → pajeon and makgeolli after the hike.

☕ Cafe locals like

서울창포원 (Seoul Iris Garden) — A large garden beside the station, nice for an easy walk before or after hiking. Avoid the rocky sections on rainy days. View on map ↗
