Bukchon Hanok Village Travel Guide: Getting There, Where to Eat and Local Tips

Last updated: November 23, 2026

한국어 · English · 日本語 · 中文

About this place

Traditional hanok houses cluster on the slope between Gyeongbokgung and Changdeokgung. People live here, so walk quietly. The view of tiled roofs and the city is the classic photo spot.

💡 Visiting tip

People live here. Around Bukchon-ro 11-gil, visiting hours are 10 a.m.–5 p.m., Monday to Saturday; Sundays are a “day of rest” for the alleys.

Local tips

🚇 Getting there

Leave Anguk Stn (Line 3) by Exit 2 and walk about 10 minutes along Bukchon-ro. There are slopes, so wear comfortable shoes.

🗺️ Suggested course

Anguk Stn → Bukchon hanok hill → Samcheong-dong → Gyeongbokgung. You can also do the reverse from Gyeongbokgung.

☕ Cafe locals like

어니언 안국 (Onion Anguk) — A bakery cafe in a hanok near Anguk Stn that locals like too. Bukchon is a residential area, so keep your voice down. View on map ↗

Please check opening status and hours on Google Maps before you go.
