Yeouido Travel Guide: Getting There, Where to Eat and Local Tips

Last updated: November 23, 2026

한국어 · English · 日本語 · 中文

About this place

Home to the National Assembly and Seoul's financial district, Yeouido shows a different face after dark. Shop at The Hyundai Seoul, then bike or picnic in Hangang Park until the sun goes down. When the Seoul International Fireworks Festival runs in late September, close to a million people gather along the Han River.

💡 Visiting tip

Close to Yeouinaru Station (Line 5) and National Assembly Station (Line 9). On big-event days like the fireworks festival, the subway gets extremely crowded, so allow extra time.

Local tips

🚇 Getting there

From Yeouinaru Stn (Line 5), Exit 3, it is a 1–2 minute walk to Hangang Park. For the National Assembly, head out from National Assembly Stn (Line 9).

🗺️ Suggested course

Shop at The Hyundai Seoul → walk or bike in Yeouido Hangang Park → catch a river cruise or sunset at the pier in the evening. Check traffic controls ahead of time during the late-September fireworks festival.

How to do it

Yeouido Hangang Park

This stretch of Hangang Park hosts major events like the spring flower festival and the Seoul International Fireworks Festival. Bike rentals and convenience stores make it easy to picnic.

The Hyundai Seoul

One of Korea's largest department stores, known for its indoor garden and striking architecture. It leans toward lifestyle and dessert brands rather than luxury, so it's fun just to browse.

63 Square and the fireworks festival

You can combine 63 Square's observatory and aquarium in one visit. On fireworks-festival days in autumn, expect bridge closures and large crowds — grab a spot near Mapo or Wonhyo Bridge early.
