Hwaseong Fortress, Suwon Travel Guide: Getting There, Where to Eat and Local Tips

Last updated: November 23, 2026

한국어 · English · 日本語 · 中文

About this place

A fortress wall built under King Jeongjo and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Reachable from Seoul by subway, it is a popular day trip, and a full walk around the wall easily takes two to three hours.

Local tips

🚇 Getting there

From Suwon Stn (Line 1; about an hour by express from Seoul Stn or Yongsan), a local bus or taxi takes 10–15 minutes to the Paldalmun gate area.

🗺️ Suggested course

Janganmun gate → follow the wall to Hwaseomun → Paldalsan → Hwaseong Haenggung → Haenggung-dong cafe street. Walking the full wall takes about 3 hours.

☕ Cafe locals like

행궁동 카페거리 (Haenggung-dong) — Haenggung-dong in front of the palace has hanok, small cafes and workshops popular with young locals. Return to Seoul by Line 1 from Suwon Stn. View on map ↗

Please check opening status and hours on Google Maps before you go.
