Hongdae Street Travel Guide: Getting There, Where to Eat and Local Tips

Last updated: November 25, 2026

한국어 · English · 日本語 · 中文

About this place

A youth district that grew around an art university, full of busking, indie music clubs and quirky shops. After dark it glows with neon and street snacks.

💡 Visiting tip

Exit 9 of Hongik Univ. Station (Line 2) leads straight into the heart of the district.

Local tips

🚇 Getting there

Exit 9 of Hongik Univ. Stn (Line 2) leads to the Hongdae Walking Street. The AREX airport railroad takes about 50 minutes from Incheon Airport.

🗺️ Suggested course

Yeonnam-dong Gyeongui Line Forest Park → Hongdae busking street → one stop on Line 2 to Hapjeong for a cafe → dinner at Mangwon Market.

☕ Cafe locals like

앤트러사이트 합정 (Anthracite Hapjeong) — A roastery cafe in a former shoe factory in Hapjeong, with an atmosphere locals enjoy. View on map ↗

Please check opening status and hours on Google Maps before you go.

How to do it

Hongdae Free Market and busking

On weekend afternoons, independent craft vendors set up a free market around Hongdae Playground, alongside street performances. Days and times shift with the season, so check on site.

Beauty shops and boutiques

Beauty chains like Olive Young, plus quirky boutiques and casual clothing shops, pack the narrow alleys — great for a slow walk-and-browse.

Indie clubs after dark

After sunset, the alleys packed with indie music clubs and bars come alive. Check each club's own channel for cover charges and show times.
