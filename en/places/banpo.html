Banpo Bridge & Han River night view Travel Guide: Getting There, Where to Eat and Local Tips

Last updated: November 25, 2026

한국어 · English · 日本語 · 中文

About this place

The easiest way to enjoy the Han River at night is Banpo Hangang Park, where the bridge's Moonlight Rainbow Fountain draws rainbows over the river with lights. Show times vary by season, so check Seoul's notices.

Local tips

🚇 Getting there

Take Express Bus Terminal Stn (Lines 3, 7, 9) toward Exit 8-1, then walk 10–15 minutes toward Banpo Hangang Park.

🗺️ Suggested course

Express Bus Terminal Stn → sunset walk in Banpo Hangang Park → Moonlight Rainbow Fountain → dinner in Seorae Village.

☕ Cafe locals like

서래마을 카페 거리 — Seorae Village near the station has quiet cafes and bakeries. Check the fountain schedule for the season. View on map ↗

Please check opening status and hours on Google Maps before you go.

How to do it

Han River night cruise

E-Land Cruise, departing from Yeouido Hangang Park pier (Yeouinaru Stn, Line 5), is the main option, with sunset, night and music cruises. Standard tickets are around ₩10,000–29,000; dinner cruises cost more. Check times and fares on the official booking site.

Moonlight Rainbow Fountain

Water arcs from both sides of Banpo Bridge, usually from spring to October only (off in winter). It stops in strong wind or rain.
