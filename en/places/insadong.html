Insadong Travel Guide: Getting There, Where to Eat and Local Tips

Last updated: November 23, 2026

한국어 · English · 日本語 · 中文

About this place

A street of traditional craft shops, tea houses and galleries. Follow the Ssamziegil alleys, then rest with traditional tea or hangwa sweets.

Local tips

🚇 Getting there

Exit 6 of Anguk Stn (Line 3) leads to the start of Insadong-gil. Jongno 3-ga Stn (Lines 1, 3, 5), Exit 5 is also close.

🗺️ Suggested course

Anguk Stn → Insadong-gil and Ssamziegil → Ikseon-dong → return from Jongno 3-ga Stn. Two to three slow hours on foot is enough.

☕ Cafe locals like

귀빈재 (Gwibinjae) — A traditional tea house in Insadong, good for ssanghwacha or jujube tea with hangwa sweets. Check the location and hours in a map app. View on map ↗

Please check opening status and hours on Google Maps before you go.
